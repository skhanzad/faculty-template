\begin{applicationdocument}{References}

\tip[Choosing and preparing referees]{Choose three or four people who
together cover your research, your teaching and your independence. A
typical set is your doctoral supervisor, your postdoctoral host, an
external collaborator, and someone who has seen you teach. Ask them four to
six weeks ahead. Send each referee the posting, your current materials, the
deadline and a few points you hope they can address. Check whether the
institution collects letters itself or contacts referees later. Do not
list anyone who has not agreed.}
